FIRST PUBLIC BUILD

29 Aug 2026 · First public-use build of the F300 Championship app.

  • Championship standings, complete race-weekend results and season calendar.
  • Miniature circuit illustrations personalise the Race Results and Calendar screens.
  • Results are ordered by Final result and can be filtered by driver.
  • Quick app-switches keep your place; after longer inactivity the app returns cleanly to Standings.
  • Home Screen installation support for iPhone, iPad and Android.
  • Offline caching allows the most recently downloaded championship data to remain available without signal.
  • F300 startup screen appears on launch and after returning from around a minute of inactivity.
  • Contact link is available for championship questions or data corrections.
  • Driver Hub testing includes profile-photo submissions, weekend results and gearing notes.
