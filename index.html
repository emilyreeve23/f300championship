RACE DAY UPDATE

6 Sep 2026 · Race-day, driver and timing improvements across the F300 app.

  • All Lap Times adds driver-by-driver lap history for supported race weekends, split by round, Heat 1, Heat 2, Heat 3 and Final.
  • Missing lap-history rounds remain visible but are greyed out until timing data is available.
  • Speedhive events can be discovered from the F300 race calendar using the circuit and race-weekend date.
  • Race Results refresh while the app is open and order drivers by the current race-weekend points.
  • Private Admin review shows timing/import issues and new Contact F300 requests.
  • My Profile includes 4-digit PIN access, profile photos and saved gearing notes.
  • New light and dark themes can be switched from the Info screen.

F300 app under development

The app is still being refined and some features may contain bugs or behave unexpectedly.

Championship data is delivered through the F300 app and may be cached on your device so the most recently downloaded information remains available when you are offline.
